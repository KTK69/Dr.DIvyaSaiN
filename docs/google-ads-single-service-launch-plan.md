# Google Ads Launch Plan
## Single-Service Growth Proposal for Dr. Divya Sai Narsingam

Prepared on: August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This proposal recommends starting Google Ads with **one high-intent service at a time**, rather than promoting the full clinic at launch.

### Recommended first service
- **Gynecomastia Reduction**

### Why start here
- Clear patient intent: people searching this service are usually already problem-aware.
- Strong commercial language exists on the site already: “gynecomastia surgery Hyderabad”, “male breast reduction Hyderabad”, and “gynecomastia cost Hyderabad”.
- Less campaign complexity than launching all cosmetic and reconstructive services at once.
- Easier to measure lead quality, cost per lead, and consultation booking rate.
- Good fit for a surgeon-led, trust-first positioning strategy.

### Primary business goal
- Generate qualified consultation leads for one profitable procedure in Hyderabad.

### Primary marketing goal
- Build a repeatable Google Ads system that can later be cloned for rhinoplasty, breast reduction, tummy tuck, and other services.

---

## 2. Why Google Ads Is the Right Starting Channel

Google Ads is the best first paid channel because it captures **existing demand**. Patients who search for terms like “gynecomastia surgery Hyderabad” or “male breast reduction near me” are much closer to booking than cold audiences on social platforms.

### Benefits of starting with Search Ads
- Reaches patients at the exact moment of intent.
- Easier to control geography and budget.
- Faster feedback than SEO.
- Cleaner attribution for calls and form leads.
- Better for a specialist practice than broad awareness campaigns.

---

## 3. Why We Should Not Launch All Services at Once

Launching all services immediately would spread budget and data too thin.

### Risks of a broad launch
- Too many keywords with too little budget.
- Lower message match between keyword, ad, and landing page.
- Harder to know which service is actually generating profitable enquiries.
- More policy-review complexity across different healthcare topics.
- More wasted spend during the learning phase.

### Better approach
- Start with one service.
- Reach stable lead flow.
- Improve landing page conversion rate.
- Scale to the next service only after performance is understood.

---

## 4. Recommended First Campaign: Gynecomastia Reduction

### Strategic rationale
Gynecomastia is a strong first campaign candidate because it usually has:
- high-intent search behavior,
- a clear symptom-to-solution journey,
- strong relevance to consultation-led selling,
- relatively focused keywords compared with broader cosmetic categories.

### Ideal patient profile
- Men in Hyderabad and nearby high-value catchments
- Often age 18–45
- Searching for correction, surgery, recovery, cost, or best doctor
- Likely to prefer privacy, expertise, and before/after confidence over “cheap” positioning

### Core offer
- Expert gynecomastia evaluation and surgery consultation with Dr. Divya Sai Narsingam
- Hospitals: AIG Hospitals, Banjara Hills and CARE Hospitals, Gachibowli
- Value proposition: specialist plastic surgeon, surgical precision, trust, discreet care

## 5. Proposed 90-Day Rollout

## Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1)

### Account setup
- Create or clean Google Ads account structure.
- Complete advertiser verification if requested by Google.
- Connect GA4 and Google Ads.
- Set up conversion tracking for:
  - form submissions,
  - click-to-call,
  - calls from ads,
  - WhatsApp click if used as a secondary lead action.

### Landing page readiness
- Use a dedicated service page for `gynecomastia-reduction`.
- Add one strong above-the-fold CTA.
- Add surgeon credentials near the top.
- Add FAQs, consultation steps, and recovery guidance.
- Add trust elements: hospital affiliations, before/after proof where compliant, patient testimonials where appropriate.
- Ensure privacy policy is live and accessible.

## Phase 2: Launch (Weeks 2–3)

### Campaign type
- **Google Search campaign only**

### Ad group structure
- Ad Group 1: gynecomastia surgery
- Ad Group 2: male breast reduction
- Ad Group 3: branded surgeon searches
- Ad Group 4: consultation / doctor intent

### Match-type approach
- Start with **phrase and exact match** for control.
- Add broad match only after conversion tracking is stable and search term quality is acceptable.

### Keyword examples
- gynecomastia surgery hyderabad
- male breast reduction hyderabad
- gynecomastia doctor hyderabad
- gynecomastia treatment hyderabad
- best doctor for gynecomastia hyderabad
- gynecomastia operation hyderabad
- gynecomastia cost hyderabad
- gynecomastia surgeon banjara hills
- gynecomastia surgeon gachibowli
- dr divya gynecomastia

### Negative keyword themes
- free
- home remedy
- exercise only
- medicine only
- gym plan
- photos only
- youtube
- reddit
- jobs
- training
- pdf
- cheap if poor-quality leads start appearing

## Phase 3: Optimisation (Weeks 4–8)

### Weekly optimisation rhythm
- Review search terms.
- Add negatives.
- Pause weak headlines.
- Improve ad strength.
- Adjust device and schedule insights.
- Review call quality, not just lead count.
- Compare form leads vs call leads.

### Key decisions during this phase
- Whether to introduce broad match
- Whether to split brand and non-brand budgets
- Whether to promote consultation CTA or phone CTA more aggressively

## Phase 4: Scale Decision (Weeks 9–12)

Scale only if the first campaign produces:
- stable lead flow,
- acceptable cost per qualified lead,
- acceptable consultation-to-surgery quality.

Then launch the next service, recommended order:
1. **Rhinoplasty**
2. **Breast Reduction**
3. **Tummy Tuck**
4. **Body Lipocontouring**

## 6. Budget Recommendation

This is a **starting test budget recommendation**, not a live CPC benchmark. Final numbers should be validated in Google Keyword Planner before launch.

### Suggested first 60-day media budget
- **INR 45,000 to INR 75,000 per month** for ad spend

### Why this range
- Gives enough spend to collect search-term and conversion data.
- Avoids underfunding the campaign during Google’s learning period.
- Keeps the test focused on one service rather than fragmenting results.

### Suggested allocation
- 80–85%: non-brand high-intent search
- 10–15%: brand protection campaign
- 5–10%: experimentation / ad asset testing

### Commercial note
- If budget is below roughly INR 1,500 per day, launch should stay tightly focused on one service and a limited geography.

## 8. Measurement Framework

### Primary KPI
- Qualified consultation leads

### Secondary KPIs
- Cost per lead
- Cost per qualified lead
- Call answer rate
- Landing page conversion rate
- Consultation booking rate
- Show-up rate
- Procedure conversion rate

### What should count as a conversion
- Form submission
- Call over a minimum duration threshold
- WhatsApp click only as a secondary conversion

### Recommended reporting cadence
- Daily: spend, leads, search term checks in launch period
- Weekly: optimisation summary
- Monthly: business-outcome report for client

## 9. Landing Page Requirements Before Spending Heavily

The current website is suitable as a starting point, but the campaign will perform better if the gynecomastia page is strengthened.

### Recommended landing page improvements
- Strong headline with location and procedure
- A short “Am I a candidate?” section
- Surgeon credentials above the fold
- Hospital names and consultation locations
- 1 primary CTA only: Book Consultation
- 1 secondary CTA: Call Now / WhatsApp
- Procedure steps and recovery summary
- FAQ section answering cost, scars, recurrence, downtime, safety
- Real trust proof: testimonials, before/after where compliant and appropriate
- Short form with fewer fields

### Ideal CTA examples
- Book a Gynecomastia Consultation
- Speak to the Surgeon’s Team
- Call for a Private Assessment

## 11. What We Should Avoid Early

### Avoid in phase 1
- Performance Max at launch
- Display remarketing for this service
- Demand Gen for this service
- Running all cosmetic services together
- Sending traffic to the homepage
- Optimising for weak conversions only
- Cheap-price messaging that may reduce lead quality

### Why
- Sensitive-health categories need cleaner targeting and cleaner intent signals.
- Search gives the best first-party demand signal.
- Early volume is less important than accurate lead quality.

## 12. Policy and Compliance Guardrails

Based on current Google Ads help and policy materials reviewed on August 1, 2026:

- Google’s healthcare and medicines policy requires healthcare advertisers and destinations to follow applicable laws and standards.
- Lead form assets require a privacy policy and a compliant account history.
- Call reporting can be enabled to track calls from ads and call assets.
- Advertiser verification is rolling out broadly and may be required.
- Google is deprecating creation of new call-only ads; responsive search ads with call assets are the safer default.
- Health-sensitive categories should be handled cautiously with personalised targeting and remarketing.

### Practical implication for this clinic
- Start with **Search only**.
- Use **responsive search ads**.
- Add **call assets** and possibly **lead form assets** only if the account is eligible and privacy requirements are met.
- Avoid aggressive audience-led retargeting in the first phase.

## 13. Success Criteria for the First 60 Days

The first campaign should be judged on these outcomes:

### Green light to scale
- Consistent qualified leads every week
- Clear search terms with strong commercial intent
- Consultation bookings from both calls and forms
- Acceptable cost per qualified lead
- No policy issues and no landing page friction

### Yellow light
- Leads are coming in, but quality is mixed
- Search terms need tightening
- Landing page conversion rate is weak
- Phone handling needs improvement

### Red light
- Low-quality leads dominate
- Most spend goes to irrelevant queries
- No consultation momentum after 4–6 weeks
- Tracking is unreliable

## 14. Client-Facing Recommendation

### Recommended next step
Launch a **single-service Google Search campaign for Gynecomastia Reduction** for 60 days with a tightly controlled Hyderabad geography, full lead tracking, and a conversion-focused landing page.
